# Current State

Leads arrive from many sources but too few convert, because three gaps sit between a lead being generated and a real conversation with an advisor: assignment, visibility and engagement. In the current flow a lead is assigned, the RM makes contact two days later, the customer's interest has gone by then, and the lead ends with no conversion.

Lead Generated
→ Lead Assigned
→ RM Contact (2 Days Later)
→ Customer Interest Lost
→ No Conversion

This is a flow problem, not a lead-volume problem. More leads or more RMs poured into the same flow lose in the same places. The argument of this paper has four steps:
1. <u>Where the lead is lost</u>. One lead's path from generation to no conversion, and the three challenges along it.
2. Why. A root cause analysis across people, process, technology and customer.
3. The response. The six-layer AI-powered customer engagement architecture, with each root cause mapped to the layer that removes it.
4. The proof. How the business will know the system caused the improvement, not the market or luck.
The aim is to fix the allocation of RM time and customer attention, not to shrink headcount or replace advisors.


Challenges:

- No centralized lead tracking & no lead prioritization, hence, leads not mapped effectively to advisors leading to delayed customer engagement
- Advisors unaware of assigned leads & Inconsistent RM follow-up
- Fragmented customer interactions & Low visibility into customer intent, hence limited personalization for conversations
 
Root Cause Analysis - 

- People: Lack of ownership
- Process: Weak lead assignment workflow
- Technology: No advisor dashboard or notifications
- Customer: Disconnected online/offline journey
