# Current State

Currently, Leads are generated through different sources but not effectively converted due to assignment, visibility & engagement gaps.

Lead Generated
→ Lead Assigned
→ RM Contact (2 Days Later)
→ Customer Interest Lost
→ No Conversion

Challenges:

- No centralized lead tracking & no lead prioritization, hence, leads not mapped effectively to advisors leading to delayed customer engagement
- Advisors unaware of assigned leads & Inconsistent RM follow-up
- Fragmented customer interactions & Low visibility into customer intent, hence limited personalization for conversations
 
Root Cause Analysis - 

- People: Lack of ownership
- Process: Weak lead assignment workflow
- Technology: No advisor dashboard or notifications
- Customer: Disconnected online/offline journey
