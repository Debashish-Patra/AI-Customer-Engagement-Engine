# Customer Journey map

Six stages, from first hearing about investing to becoming an active, self-directed customer — mapped against what the customer does, feels, and hits friction on at each point.

<img width="1275" height="672" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/60c4c89c-55fe-4a40-a93c-8f959a8cff6e" />

The details for the stages are mentioned below - 

| Stages          | Awareness                                                               | Consideration                                                                                               | Application & Verification                                                                      | Activation & Funding                                                                     | First Trade & Engagement                                                             | Ongoing Engagement & Growth                                                                                                                                    |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Customer Action | Discovers the need to invest                                            | Compares features, reviews feedback, and checks account-opening fees                                        | Submits mobile number, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and completes KYC                            | Receives login credentials and transfers initial trading funds                           | Places the first stock/mutual fund order and explores educational tools              | Reviews portfolio performance and explores new products (SIP, F&O, insurance) at inconsistent intervals                                                        |
| Touchpoints     | Social media ads, YouTube finance videos, Google search, referral links | Broker website, app store reviews, Reddit threads, pricing comparison pages                                 | Mobile app, web signup portal, Digilocker, e-Sign gateway                                       | Welcome email/SMS, app dashboard, net banking / UPI payment gateway                      | Trading dashboard, push notifications, customer support helpdesk, learning center    | Mobile app, SIP mandates, RM check-in calls, research reports                                                                                                  |
| Pain Points     | Information overload                                                    | Hidden charges, confusing brokerage fee structures;<br>Negative reviews about app crashes / customer support | Document upload failures<br>Digi locker sync errors<br>KYC failures<br>Delayed account approval | Payment gateway transaction failures                                                     | Confusing UI for order placement;<br>Unresponsive chat support                        | Uncertainty on next investment decisions;<br>Inconsistent recommendations                                                                                       |
| Opportunities   | Jargon-free comparison blogs;<br>Transparent fee calculators             | Prominent zero-brokerage highlights;<br>Upfront AMC disclosures;<br>Live chat assistance                      | Auto-fetch via Digilocker;<br>Instant PAN verification;<br>24/7 automated video KYC               | Instant UPI fund deposits;<br>Guided in-app walkthroughs;<br>Zero-balance readiness prompt | Gamified first-trade rewards;<br>Intuitive tooltips;<br>Proactive educational webinars | Personalized portfolio insights & rebalancing nudges;<br>SIP / goal-based investment prompts;<br>RM check-ins for high-AUM clients;<br>Loyalty & referral rewards |
