Six stages, from first hearing about investing to becoming an active, self-directed customer — mapped against what the customer does, feels, and hits friction on at each point.

<img width="1275" height="672" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/60c4c89c-55fe-4a40-a93c-8f959a8cff6e" />

| Stages | Awareness | Consideration | Application & Verification | Activation & Funding | First Trade & Engagement | Ongoing Engagement & Growth |
| ------ | --------- | ------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Customer Action | Discovers the need to invest | Compares features, reviews feedback, and checks account-opening fees | Submits mobile number, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, and completes KYC | Receives login credentials and transfers initial trading funds | Places the first stock/mutual fund order and explores educational tools | Reviews portfolio performance and explores new products (SIP, F&O, insurance) at inconsistent intervals |
