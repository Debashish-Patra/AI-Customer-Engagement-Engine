# Engagement Orchestration Layer

Engagement Orchestration Layer is the product capability that coordinates every other component of the AI engagement stack into one continuous customer experience. Positioned plainly: it is the central nervous system that decides when, why, how, and through which channel a customer should be engaged across the acquisition journey.

Every customer receives relevant, timely, and personalized engagement from the moment intent is expressed until successful RM handoff. The layer dynamically coordinates AI agents, communication channels, customer intelligence, and RM workflows into one seamless journey — so the customer experiences a single continuous conversation, never a set of disconnected touches from different systems.

| Capability                    | Product question                            | Role                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Trigger Management            | When should engagement begin?               | Listens for customer events (lead submitted, app installed, KYC incomplete, website visit) and decides whether engagement is required right now                                          |
| Journey Orchestration         | What should happen next?                    | Selects the right action (WhatsApp, email, push, AI conversation, RM assignment, or wait) and sequences it into a continuous journey, not a single blast                                 |
| Conversational Intelligence   | How do we hold the conversation?            | The agentic WhatsApp layer — understands intent, retrieves context, reasons, generates a response, and takes action, rather than matching a question to a predefined answer              |
| RAG-Based Response Automation | What do we say, and can we trust it?        | Grounds every response in real knowledge sources (FAQs, brokerage plans, KYC policies, compliance guidelines) before the LLM answers — a grounded response instead of a hallucinated one |
| RM Collaboration              | Who should act next, and with what context? | Hands the RM a full package — customer profile, intent summary, conversation history, lead score, recommended next action — instead of a cold lead                                       |

1. Trigger Management - Customer actions generate events — lead submitted, app installed, demat journey started, WhatsApp message received, campaign click, website visit, portfolio created, KYC incomplete. The orchestration engine listens for these and decides whether engagement is required. Without this capability, engagement stays batch-driven and reactive — the exact failure mode this layer exists to remove.
2. Journey Orchestration - This is where product strategy becomes visible. Take a customer who downloads the app: the layer chooses among WhatsApp message, email, push notification, AI conversation, RM assignment, or waiting — and the choice isn't made once. A full journey looks like: AI welcome message → customer responds → intent assessment → lead qualification → product recommendation → RM handoff → RM follow-up. The customer should feel they are in one continuous conversation throughout.
3. Conversational Intelligence - A traditional chatbot maps a customer question directly to a predefined answer. An agentic system instead understands intent, retrieves context, reasons, generates a response, and takes action — it progresses the journey rather than just answering it. For example, when a customer says "I want to start investing," the agent doesn't return a canned reply — it asks whether they're investing for wealth creation, trading, or retirement, then asks if they have a demat account. It is qualifying the lead, not simply responding to it.
4. RAG-Based Response Automation - Without retrieval, an LLM produces a generic answer straight from its training. With RAG, a customer query triggers knowledge retrieval, context injection, and only then a response — grounded in product FAQs, brokerage plans, research methodologies, onboarding processes, KYC policies, customer communications, and compliance guidelines. The product outcome is a grounded response instead of a hallucinated one, which is not optional in financial services.
5. RM Collaboration Layer - Most chatbot projects fail by trying to automate everything. The correct framing is augmentation: AI handles engagement, the RM handles the relationship. Before any handoff, the RM receives a package — customer profile, intent summary, conversation history, lead score, recommended next action, product interest. Concretely, after a 15-message AI conversation, an RM might see: interested in equity investing, income ₹15L+, first-time investor, requested callback, lead score 92. That context is what turns a cold lead into a productive first call.

The AI Decisioning Platform outputs a decision — engage, wait, escalate, or assign to RM — plus the scores behind it. Journey Orchestration is the mechanism that turns that decision into an actual, sequenced customer interaction.
