# AI Powered Customer Engagement

Every customer who shows interest should be met with the right message, at the right time, through the right channel, with the right human touch, and the business should be able to prove it worked. <br>
An AI-powered customer engagement platform thus, is designed to improve lead conversion by reducing response latency, personalizing customer interactions and orchestrating next-best actions across the acquisition funnel.

### THE PROBLEM<br>
Leads come in from many sources but don’t convert. They are assigned late (RM contact about 2 days later), advisors don’t know what’s assigned to them, follow-up is inconsistent, and the online and offline journeys are disconnected. By the time someone calls, interest has gone.

### THE IDEA<br>
Replace manual, delayed, one-size-fits-all follow-up with a system that decides who to reach, with what, through which channel and when. People step in where judgement and trust matter. The aim is the right customer, message, channel and time.

### THE 6 LAYERS 

<p align="center"><img width="900" height="482" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cc213d93-1a1b-4401-82b7-b96b68d442d3" />

| # | Layer                    | What it does                                                                                                                                                  |
|---|--------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Lead Sources             | Captures leads from every channel into one tracked, deduplicated pipeline                                                                                     |
| 2 | Customer Intelligence    | Builds one customer view (customer_id): profile, behaviour, intent, stage                                                                                     |
| 3 | AI Decisioning           | Decides the next best action and priority (decision_id), within governance guardrails                                                                         |
| 4 | Engagement Orchestration | Owns when and how: channel, timing, sequencing, frequency (journey_id, touch_id)                                                                              |
| 5 | Human Engagement         | Routes hot or complex leads to advisors with context, dashboards and alerts, and trains them                                                                  |
| 6 | Business Outcomes        | Proves it worked, with net new AUM as the proposed North Star, plus engagement, advisor productivity and AI effectiveness metrics, validated by holdout tests |

The solution combined:
- Customer Data Platform (CDP)
- Propensity Models
- Decision Engine
- Agentic WhatsApp Assistant
- Relationship Manager Workflow

Each layer has one job and hands to the next. Layer 3 is highlighted because it is where the system decides what happens to each customer. The design rests on five principles: one customer, one journey; AI for the routine and people for the advice; one owner per decision; consent and governance built in; and measured and proven results.

### THE UNDERLYING PRINCIPLE<br>
One owner per decision. Shared identifiers across layers. Humans own handoff thresholds. Guardrails and risks are built in. Impact is proven through incrementality, not assumed.

<p align="center"><img width="900" height="355" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dd7b6f77-1649-48fd-a22d-4fb8ce57dd8a" />

Take a customer who messages "I want to start investing". After the safety checks, Layer 3 picks one of four actions: engage, wait, escalate or assign to an RM, the last two both leading to the RM branch. The RM receives a summary of the goal, interest and history with a recommended next action. The result is tied back to the decision that produced it.

<p align="center"><img width="900" height="418" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d1a99635-a83b-4df1-9112-3a159c14397a" />

8 decisions across 6 layers - When and how to contact belong to Layer 4; what to say is split between Layers 3 and 4; and Layer 5 sets the handoff thresholds. Four identifiers carry the thread across layers: customer_id (Layer 2), decision_id (Layer 3), journey_id (Layer 4) and touch_id (Layers 4 and 5), so any outcome can be traced back to the decision and message that produced it.

<p align="center"><img width="900" height="402" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a2724c2b-13f9-4076-9ae4-770603090758" />

Guardrails are checked in the flow, not added afterwards. The full guide maps nine risks to the guardrail each relies on, including wrong AI answers, unfair or drifting models, over-contact, a failing decision layer, and franchise and offline channels left outside the flow.

### OUTCOMES<br>
These are valued in three tiers: customer (speed, relevance), business (conversion, AUM, advisor productivity) and strategic (scalable engagement capability).

<p align="center"><img width="900" height="435" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3c2450da-2643-4db6-84d3-b7e5a0bd4b8e" />

A result counts only if the comparison with customers the system left alone shows the system caused it. Net new AUM is proposed as the North Star, with opt-outs and complaints beside it as guardrails.
